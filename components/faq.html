FAQS

Shipping typically takes 3-5 business days for domestic orders and 7-14 business days for international orders.

Once your order has shipped, you will receive an email with a tracking number. You can use this number on our website or the carrier's website to track your package.

Please visit our Returns & Exchanges page for detailed instructions and to initiate a return or exchange request.

If your order has not yet shipped, please contact our customer support immediately to update your delivery address or contact information. Once shipped, changes may not be possible.
